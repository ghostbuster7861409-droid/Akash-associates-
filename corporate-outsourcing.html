Turnkey Corporate Outsourcing Services in India

Operating an in-house finance, bookkeeping, and statutory compliance department in India involves substantial operational overhead, talent retention difficulties, and constant exposure to statutory penalties due to fast-evolving tax and labor regulations.

At Singh Akash and Associates, we function as your dedicated Virtual Finance & Secretarial Office. Led by Founding Partner Akash Singh, our multidisciplinary team handles day-to-day transaction processing, employee payroll disbursements, statutory deductions (PF, ESI, TDS), and annual corporate filings with uncompromising accuracy and confidentiality.

Our Specialized Outsourcing Practice Areas

Full-Cycle Accounts & Bookkeeping Outsourcing Virtual Finance Office

End-to-end ledger management, voucher processing, bank reconciliations (BRS), and monthly profit & loss and balance sheet preparations adhering to Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS / AS).

  • Daily transaction entry, sales invoicing, vendor bill verification, and expense classification.
  • Monthly bank reconciliations and inter-company ledger reconciliations.
  • Accounts Payable (AP) three-way matching and Accounts Receivable (AR) aging schedules.
  • Month-end closing reports, trial balance generation, and executive MIS dashboards.

Payroll Processing & Labour Law Compliances Zero Default Guarantee

Confidential, automated payroll execution ensuring accurate salary computations, leave encashment calculations, and timely remittance of all statutory employee welfare dues.

  • Monthly gross-to-net salary calculations, computerized pay slip generation, and direct bank disbursement files.
  • Employee Provident Fund (EPF): Monthly ECR challan generation and filing by the 15th of each month.
  • Employee State Insurance (ESI): Monthly contribution computation, return filing, and IP card assistance.
  • Professional Tax (PT): State-specific calculations and monthly/annual PT return filings.
  • TDS on Salary (Section 192): Quarterly Form 24Q filing, tax deduction proof verifications, and annual Form 16 issuance.

Secretarial Compliances & Annual ROC Filings MCA V3 Portal

Guaranteed adherence to the mandatory corporate governance guidelines under the Companies Act, 2013, protecting directors from disqualification and companies from hefty daily additional fees.

  • Annual Filing: Preparation and filing of Form AOC-4 (Financial Statements) and Form MGT-7 (Annual Return).
  • Board Governance: Drafting notices, agendas, board resolutions, and drafting minutes for Board Meetings & AGM.
  • Director KYC: Annual DIR-3 KYC filing for all active DIN holders before September 30.
  • Statutory Registers: Maintenance of registers of members, debenture holders, directors, and share transfers.
  • Changes in registered office, director appointment/resignation (DIR-12), and increase in authorized share capital (SH-7).

Legal, Commercial Agreement & Deed Drafting

Drafting legally resilient contracts that protect your commercial interests, define liability boundaries, and anticipate dispute resolution protocols.

  • Shareholder Agreements (SHA), Share Purchase Agreements (SPA), and Founders' Agreements.
  • Master Service Agreements (MSA), Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA), and Vendor Service Contracts.
  • Employment Contracts, Consultancy Agreements, and IP Assignment Deeds.
  • Commercial Lease Agreements, Sub-leases, and Licensor-Licensee Agreements.

Corporate Litigation Support & Legal Audit Documentation

Providing technical accounting and corporate law backing during commercial disputes, regulatory inquiries, and National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT) proceedings.

  • Formulation of legal responses to Show Cause Notices issued by the Registrar of Companies (ROC) or Regional Director (RD).
  • Compounding of offenses under the Companies Act 2013 before NCLT or RD.
  • Forensic document compilation, financial impact modeling, and expert witness documentation.

Cloud Accounting Migration & ERP Implementation

Transitioning legacy desktop accounting to secure, real-time cloud ERP platforms with automated banking feeds and role-based data permissions.
