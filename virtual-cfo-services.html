Strategic Virtual CFO & Transaction Advisory in India

As companies scale from early revenues to institutional funding rounds or multi-crore turnovers, basic bookkeeping is no longer sufficient. Enterprise growth requires strategic cash flow forecasting, unit economics optimization, investor reporting, and rigorous corporate governance. However, engaging a full-time, veteran Chief Financial Officer often demands prohibitive compensation packages exceeding ₹40–60 Lakhs annually.

Singh Akash and Associates bridges this critical gap. Our Virtual CFO & Advisory practice, personally directed by Founding Partner Akash Singh, delivers institutional-grade financial stewardship, investor-ready data rooms, and strategic boardroom guidance at a fraction of the cost.

Our Core Advisory Practice Areas

Virtual CFO Retainer Services C-Suite Partnership

Comprehensive financial stewardship acting as your strategic executive partner, guiding capital allocation, runway extension, and executive decisions.

  • Rolling 13-Week Cash Flow Models: Real-time liquidity forecasting, working capital optimization, and burn-rate reduction.
  • Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A): Dynamic annual budgeting, revenue scenario modeling, and gross margin variance analysis.
  • Board & Investor Relations: Monthly executive MIS packs, investor update decks, and direct attendance at Board meetings.
  • Banking negotiations for working capital credit limits, Letter of Credit (LC), and Bank Guarantees (BG).

Financial, Tax & Operational Due Diligence (FDD) Buy-Side & Sell-Side

Pre-investment and pre-acquisition verification for venture capital funds, private equity investors, and acquiring corporations to uncover hidden balance sheet liabilities and off-balance sheet risks.

  • Quality of Earnings (QoE): Normalized EBITDA adjustments, recurring revenue verification, and non-arm's length transaction identification.
  • Tax Due Diligence: Review of past Income Tax, GST, TDS, and PF assessments to quantify contingent tax exposures.
  • Working Capital peg calculations and net debt verification for Purchase Price Adjustments.
  • Virtual Data Room (VDR) curation and investor inquiry coordination.

Business Valuation (Income Tax, FEMA & Companies Act) Statutory Reports

Defensible, rigorous valuation reports required for funding rounds, share transfers, and cross-border FDI allotments in compliance with statutory valuation rules.

  • Discounted Cash Flow (DCF): Compliant with Rule 11UA of the Income Tax Rules and RBI FEMA valuation norms.
  • Net Asset Value (NAV) and Comparable Companies Multiple (CCM / EV-to-EBITDA) methods.
  • Registered Valuer (IBBI) and SEBI Merchant Banker coordination where statutory certifications are required.

Employee Stock Option Plans (ESOP) Framing & Valuations

Attract and retain Tier-1 talent by designing equity incentive schemes aligned with Indian corporate law and tax optimization.

  • Drafting comprehensive ESOP Schemes compliant with Section 62(1)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013.
  • Structuring vesting schedules (cliff period, milestone-based vesting, liquidity events).
  • Fair market valuation of shares at grant date and exercise date for perquisite tax calculations.
  • Accounting for employee share-based payments under Ind AS 102.

Fraud Detection, Forensic Review & Risk Management

Investigating suspected financial misappropriation, procurement kickbacks, ghost vendors, and inventory diversion.

  • Forensic examination of bank statements, vendor ledgers, and digital transaction trails.
  • Whistleblower complaint investigations and evidence documentation for legal proceedings.
  • Design of internal anti-fraud controls, dual authorization matrices, and procurement verification SOPs.

Resident Director Services for Foreign Companies Section 149(3) Compliance

Under Section 149(3) of the Companies Act, 2013, every Indian company must have at least one director who resides in India for ≥ 182 days in the financial year.

  • Provision of qualified, experienced resident Indian professional directors.
  • Non-executive representation ensuring seamless bank account openings and MCA V3 filings.
  • Complete indemnification structuring and corporate governance oversight.

NGO, Section 8 Company & Trust Advisory (12A / 80G / CSR)

End-to-end regulatory support for non-profit entities, philanthropic trusts, and Section 8 companies seeking tax exemptions and corporate CSR funding.

  • Sections 12A & 80G: Permanent registration and re-validation on the Income Tax portal in Form 10A/10AB.
  • CSR Compliance: Guiding corporate donors and recipient NGOs on Section 135 CSR schedule VII expenditures.
  • FCRA (Foreign Contribution Regulation Act) registration advisory and annual Form FC-4 returns.
