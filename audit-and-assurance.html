Independent Audit & Assurance Services in India

In today's stringent corporate governance and regulatory landscape, audits are far more than a mandatory statutory box-ticking exercise. An independent, high-integrity audit delivers essential stakeholder confidence, protects promoter equity, reassures banking institutions and investors, and exposes underlying operational vulnerabilities.

At Singh Akash and Associates, our assurance practice is anchored in strict adherence to the Standards on Auditing (SAs) issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) and National Financial Reporting Authority (NFRA) guidelines. Every audit is conducted under the direct supervision of Founding Partner Akash Singh.

Our Core Audit & Assurance Practice Areas

Statutory Financial Statement Audit (Companies Act 2013) Mandatory for All Companies

Independent verification of balance sheets, profit & loss accounts, and cash flow statements in accordance with Section 139–143 of the Companies Act, 2013. We examine true and fair view representation, depreciation accounting, related party transactions (Section 188), and CARO 2020 reporting mandates.

Tax Audit under Section 44AB (Income Tax Act, 1961) Form 3CA / 3CB & 3CD

Mandatory for business entities exceeding ₹1 Crore turnover (or ₹10 Crores where cash transactions are ≤ 5%) and professionals exceeding ₹50 Lakhs gross receipts. We perform thorough verification of disallowances under Section 40(a)(ia), 43B statutory dues, MSME delayed payment disclosures (Section 43B(h)), and depreciation schedules.

Internal & Management Process Audits

Systematic evaluation of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), revenue leakage points, procurement-to-pay cycles, and operational efficiencies. We provide C-suites and audit committees with pragmatic, risk-mitigating recommendations.

Physical Stock, Inventory & Fixed Assets Verification

Comprehensive on-site physical stock counts, inventory valuation (AS-2 / Ind AS 2), dead stock reconciliation, and fixed asset register (FAR) physical tagging for bank credit limits (CC / OD limits) and statutory year-end closures.

Internal Financial Controls over Financial Reporting (IFC/ICFR)

Evaluating the design adequacy and operational effectiveness of financial control mechanisms under Section 134(5)(e) and Section 143(3)(i) of the Companies Act, eliminating risk of fraudulent reporting and accounting lapses.

Unique Document Identification Number (UDIN) Certification

Every audit report, net worth certificate, turnover attestation, and tax certification issued by our firm carries an official 18-digit UDIN generated via the ICAI Portal, providing clients, tax authorities, and lenders with instant online verification.

Under Section 139 of the Companies Act, 2013, every private limited company, public company, and Section 8 company incorporated in India is legally mandated to appoint an independent statutory auditor and get its annual financial statements audited, regardless of whether it conducted active business operations or had zero turnover during the financial year.
For commercial businesses, tax audit is mandatory if gross turnover or sales exceed ₹1 Crore. However, this threshold is increased to ₹10 Crores if cash receipts and cash payments do not exceed 5% of aggregate transactions. For professionals (e.g. consultants, IT architects, doctors), tax audit is mandatory if gross professional receipts exceed ₹50 Lakhs (or ₹75 Lakhs under Section 44ADA presumptive limits).
Companies (Auditor's Report) Order, 2020 (CARO 2020) requires extensive statutory reporting on 21 distinct clauses including inventory verification, physical fixed asset discrepancies, loans to related parties, Benami property proceedings, undisclosed income surrender, and whistleblower complaints. Small private companies (paid-up capital ≤ ₹4 Crores, turnover ≤ ₹40 Crores, borrowings ≤ ₹1 Crore) are exempt from CARO.
