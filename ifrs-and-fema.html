Global Reporting, Transfer Pricing & FEMA Advisory

Operating across international jurisdictions introduces intricate regulatory scrutiny from both the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) under the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999, and the Directorate of International Taxation under the Income Tax Act, 1961. Cross-border intra-group transactions, overseas equity allotments, and foreign vendor remittances require precision structuring to prevent severe compounding fines and double taxation.

At Singh Akash and Associates, our International Tax & Regulatory practice assists foreign corporations, Indian exporters, venture-backed tech startups, and high-net-worth NRIs in maintaining complete cross-border regulatory compliance.

Our Core International Advisory Offerings

Transfer Pricing Studies & Form 3CEB Certification Arm's Length Principle

Under Section 92 to 92F of the Income Tax Act, any international transaction or specified domestic transaction (SDT) between associated enterprises must adhere strictly to the arm's length standard.

  • Form 3CEB: Mandatory annual Chartered Accountant certification filed before October 31.
  • Transfer Pricing Study Report: Detailed functional, asset, and risk (FAR) analysis with economic benchmarking using Prowess and Capitaline databases.
  • Most Appropriate Method (MAM) determination: TNMM, CUP, Resale Price, Cost Plus, or Profit Split Method.
  • Master File (Form 3CEAA) and Country-by-Country (CbC) Reporting (Form 3CEAD) for qualifying global enterprises.

FEMA Advisory, FDI & Form FC-GPR Reporting to RBI FIRMS Portal Filing

Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) into Indian private entities is governed by RBI Master Directions and FEMA Regulations. Capital inflow must be reported in accordance with strict statutory timelines.

  • Sectoral cap verification and checking Automatic Route vs. Government Approval Route criteria.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) and Know Your Customer (KYC) verification from Authorized Dealer (AD) Category-I banks.
  • Form FC-GPR: Mandatory electronic filing on RBI's FIRMS portal within 30 days of equity or CCD share allotment.
  • Form FC-TRS: Reporting transfer of capital instruments between residents and non-residents within 60 days of transfer.

Foreign Remittance Certifications (Form 15CA & Form 15CB) Section 195 Compliance

Banks in India are strictly prohibited from processing outward foreign currency remittances unless accompanied by statutory tax clearances under Section 195 of the Income Tax Act.

  • Form 15CB: Independent Chartered Accountant certificate verifying withholding tax rates, DTAA benefits, and royalty/FTS taxability.
  • Form 15CA: Electronic undertaking filed on the Income Tax e-filing portal across Parts A, B, C, or D.
  • Verification of Permanent Establishment (PE) exposure and Tax Residency Certificates (TRC) with Form 10F.
  • Fast-track same-day certification for urgent software import, royalty, advisory fee, and import remittances.

Annual Return on Foreign Liabilities and Assets (FLA Return)

Mandatory annual compliance for all Indian companies and LLPs that have received Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) or made Overseas Direct Investment (ODI).

  • Mandatory electronic submission on the RBI's FLAIR portal on or before July 15 each financial year.
  • Detailed reporting of foreign equity holding percentages, retained earnings, reserves, and foreign financial liabilities.
  • Prevention of RBI compounding penalties and restriction of future foreign share allotments.

IFRS & Ind AS Financial Reporting, Gap Analysis & Conversion

Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS) are converged with International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) and are mandatory for listed companies and unlisted entities with net worth ≥ ₹250 Crores.

  • Comprehensive GAAP convergence and conversion from Indian GAAP (AS) to Ind AS / IFRS.
  • Technical advisory on complex standards: Ind AS 115 (Revenue Recognition), Ind AS 116 (Lease Accounting), and Ind AS 109 (Financial Instruments).
  • Consolidated Financial Statements (CFS) for multinational groups with multi-currency subsidiaries.

Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTAA) & Cross-Border Structuring

Leveraging India's bilateral tax treaties with over 85 countries (USA, UK, Singapore, UAE, Germany, Mauritius) to eliminate dual taxation on cross-border income flows.

  • Determining beneficial tax rates under Section 90 vs. domestic Income Tax Act provisions.
  • Foreign Tax Credit (FTC) advisory and mandatory Form 67 filings for resident Indian entities and professionals.
  • Withholding tax structuring on software royalties, technical services (FTS), and dividend repatriation.
