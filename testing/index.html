Knowledge & Due Dates

Statutory Compliance Due Dates

Key compliance deadlines for businesses, LLPs and individual taxpayers.

Statutory Body Form / Return Frequency Standard Statutory Due Date Action
GST (CBIC) GSTR-1 (Outward Supplies Statement) Monthly (Turnover > 5 Cr) / Quarterly 11th of every month / 13th following quarter Enquire →
GST (CBIC) GSTR-3B (Summary Return & Tax Settlement) Monthly / Quarterly 20th of every month (or 22nd/24th under QRMP) Enquire →
Income Tax TDS / TCS Deposit (Challan ITNS 281) Monthly 7th of subsequent month (30th April for March) Enquire →
Income Tax Quarterly TDS Returns (24Q / 26Q / 27Q) Quarterly 31st of the month following quarter end Enquire →
Income Tax Advance Tax Instalments (Corporate & Individual) Quarterly 15th June, 15th Sept, 15th Dec, 15th March Enquire →
Income Tax Income Tax Return (ITR) (Non-Audit Cases) Annual 31st July of the Assessment Year Enquire →
MCA / ROC DIR-3 KYC (Director Identification KYC) Annual 30th September of the Financial Year Enquire →
