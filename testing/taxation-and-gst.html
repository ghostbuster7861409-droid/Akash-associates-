Direct & Indirect Taxation Services in India

India's taxation ecosystem is multifaceted and continuously shifting, characterized by automated faceless assessment regimes, computerized data analytics cross-matching GST with Income Tax, and complex international treaty provisions. Navigating this demands proactive tax strategy rather than reactive end-of-year calculations.

Led by Founding Partner Akash Singh, Singh Akash and Associates delivers comprehensive direct and indirect tax governance designed to eliminate penalties, optimize allowable deductions, and safeguard companies during department scrutiny.

Our Core Taxation Offerings

Corporate Income Tax Planning & Return Filing

Strategic corporate income tax computations for Domestic Companies (Section 115BAA @ 22% and Section 115BAB @ 15%), LLPs, and Partnerships. We calculate advance tax instalments quarterly (15% June, 45% Sept, 75% Dec, 100% March) to prevent Section 234B/234C interest penalties and manage complex depreciation claims under Section 32.

Goods & Services Tax (GST) Returns & Annual Audit GSTR-1, 3B, 9 & 9C

Complete GST compliance suite including monthly outward supply reporting (GSTR-1), monthly tax liability and ITC utilization (GSTR-3B), comprehensive 2B/2A automated invoice matching to protect against Section 16(2)(aa) input credit blockages, and preparation of Annual GST Returns (GSTR-9) and Reconciliation Statements (GSTR-9C).

GST Refunds: Export of Goods/Services & Inverted Duty Direct Bank Disbursement

We specialize in unlocking accumulated input tax credit for software exporters, IT/ITeS firms, export houses, and manufacturing entities facing inverted tax rates. We file and follow through on RFD-01 refund claims through to final sanction order (RFD-06).

Capital Gains Advisory on Real Estate, Equity & Startups

Optimizing Short-Term and Long-Term Capital Gains (STCG & LTCG) on property sale, startup stock options (ESOPs), unlisted equity shares, and mutual funds. We design reinvestment strategies under Sections 54, 54EC (Capital Gain Bonds), and 54F to legally minimize liability.

Expatriate & Cross-Border Non-Resident (NRI) Taxation

Advising NRIs, foreign expats working in India, and Indian residents with overseas assets (Schedule FA). Determining tax residency status under Section 6 of the Income Tax Act, Double Tax Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) relief under Section 90, and issuing Form 15CA/15CB remittance certificates.

Departmental Scrutiny Defense & CIT(A) Appellate Representation

Handling notices under Section 148 (Reassessment), Section 143(2) (Complete Scrutiny), and Section 142(1). We draft detailed factual submissions for Faceless Assessment Units (FAU) and represent clients before the Commissioner of Income Tax (Appeals) and GST Appellate Authorities.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Under Section 16(2)(aa) of the CGST Act, taxpayers are strictly prohibited from availing Input Tax Credit unless the supplier has uploaded the corresponding invoice in their GSTR-1 and it appears in the buyer's auto-generated GSTR-2B. Claiming mismatched or unreflected ITC triggers automated DRC-01B notices and Section 50 interest penalties @ 18% p.a.
When exports are executed under a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) without payment of IGST, accumulated unutilized input tax credits can be claimed as cash refund under Rule 89(4). With our automated ledger reconciliation and statutory CA certification, refund applications (Form RFD-01) typically receive initial sanction within 15–20 working days.
Advance tax is payable in four quarterly instalments: 15% on or before June 15, 45% by September 15, 75% by December 15, and 100% by March 15. Deferral or short-payment attracts mandatory interest penal charges under Section 234C (1% per month for the delay period) and Section 234B (1% per month from April 1 until full tax discharge).
