How to Register a Company in India

It is often said that "the journey of a thousand miles begins with one step," and for entrepreneurs, founders, and corporate leaders, that first step is "Company Formation." In India, this is an automated, centralized statutory process handled through the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) under the Companies Act, 2013.

Although the government has streamlined this process through the SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically Plus) integrated system, there remains critical legal paperwork involved in drafting the Memorandum of Association (MoA), Articles of Association (AoA), director disclosures, and capital structuring. At Singh Akash and Associates, under the direct stewardship of Founding Partner Akash Singh, we ensure that your company's foundation is legally watertight so that you can concentrate on your core business vision.

What Are the Different Business Structures in India?

Choosing the right legal structure dictates your compliance burden, tax exposure, investor appeal, and liability protection. Below are the primary legal business entities available in India:

Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) Most Popular for Startups

The most established corporate vehicle for high-growth ventures, venture-backed startups, and family-owned enterprises. Offers complete limited liability protection and distinct corporate personality.

  • Requires a minimum of 2 directors and 2 shareholders (up to a maximum of 200).
  • Essential for raising venture capital (VC), angel investment, or bank debt funding.
  • Separate legal entity with perpetual succession and transferable equity shares.

Limited Liability Partnership (LLP) Low Compliance Overhead

Governed under the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008. Blends the organizational flexibility of a partnership with the limited liability safeguards of a corporate body.

  • Requires minimum 2 partners; no upper limit on the maximum number of partners.
  • No mandatory statutory audit unless annual turnover exceeds ₹40 Lakhs or contribution exceeds ₹25 Lakhs.
  • Exemption from Dividend Distribution Tax (DDT) and Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT) benefits.

Start-up India (DPIIT Recognition) Govt Tax Holidays

Official accreditation by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) under the Government of India flagship initiative.

  • Section 80-IAC Tax Holiday: 100% income tax exemption for 3 consecutive years out of the first 10 years.
  • Section 56(2)(viib) Exemption: Total relief from "Angel Tax" on capital raised above fair market value.
  • Fast-track patent and trademark filing with an 80% statutory government fee rebate.

Foreign Subsidiaries & Inbound Setup FDI & RBI Compliance

For foreign multinational corporations, US/UK/Singapore holding companies setting up wholly-owned subsidiaries (WOS), liaison offices, or branch offices in India.

  • 100% Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) permitted under automatic route for most commercial sectors.
  • Complete RBI / FEMA reporting compliance, including Form FC-GPR filing upon share allotment.
  • Resident Indian Director provision and authorized representative support.

One Person Company (OPC) & Public Limited Companies

Whether you are a solo entrepreneur wanting corporate status with full control (OPC) or an enterprise looking to raise public capital with 7+ shareholders and listed options (Public Ltd), we handle end-to-end charters.

MSME (Udyam), Trademark & Partnership Deeds

Beyond MCA incorporation, we secure your intellectual property through Trademark Class searches and filings, obtain Udyam MSME certification for priority sector lending, and register traditional partnership deeds.

The 4-Step Incorporation Process in India

With our streamlined digital execution, Singh Akash and Associates typically completes incorporation within 7 to 10 business days, subject to MCA portal processing:

1
Digital Signatures (DSC)

Obtaining Class-3 Digital Signature Certificates for all proposed directors and shareholders.

2
Name Reservation (RUN)

Filing Part A of SPICe+ for corporate name availability and trademark check.

3
SPICe+ Part B Filing

Drafting e-MoA, e-AoA, AGILE-PRO-S (EPFO, ESIC, Bank A/c, GSTIN), and INC-9 declarations.

4
Certificate of Incorporation

Allotment of CIN, PAN, TAN, and formal issuance of Certificate of Incorporation (COI).

Essential Documents Checklist

  • PAN Card copy of all Directors / Partners
  • Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID copy
  • Latest Bank Statement / Electricity Bill (< 2 months)
  • Passport-sized photographs of promoters
  • Registered Office Proof (Electricity / Water bill)
  • Rent Agreement & No-Objection Certificate (NOC)
